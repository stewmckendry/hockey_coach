## 🏒 YOUR JOB (Pick Your Position)

### ⚡ FORWARDS - CENTER
📍 **WHERE YOU START:** Middle of the ice, below the puck
🎯 **WHAT YOU DO:**
1. Cover the slot (middle area)
2. Help defense down low
3. First forward back to help

⚡ **THUNDER TIP:** "You're the third defender - stay low!"
❌ **DON'T DO THIS:** Don't chase behind the net

### ⚡ FORWARDS - WINGERS  
📍 **WHERE YOU START:** Cover the point (defenseman at blue line)
🎯 **WHAT YOU DO:**
1. Stick on the point man
2. Block shots from point
3. Stay high unless puck goes to corner

⚡ **THUNDER TIP:** "Point man is YOUR man - don't leave him!"
❌ **DON'T DO THIS:** Don't go into the corners

### 🛡️ DEFENSE
📍 **WHERE YOU START:** In front of our net
🎯 **WHAT YOU DO:**
1. One D battles in corner
2. Other D protects the net
3. Switch when puck moves

⚡ **THUNDER TIP:** "Always have one D in front!"
❌ **DON'T DO THIS:** Both D can't chase the puck

### 🥅 GOALIE
📍 **WHERE YOU ARE:** In your crease
🎯 **WHAT YOU DO:**
1. Track the puck
2. Call out threats ("Back door!")

⚡ **THUNDER TIP:** "Be loud - you see everything!"
