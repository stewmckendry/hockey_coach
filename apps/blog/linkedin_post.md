# LinkedIn Post - From the Rink to the Terminal

Took the summer off from writing to enjoy ball diamonds and provincials. But the AI landscape doesn't pause—agents became the next frontier and MCP servers flooded in as every company jumped on the bandwagon.

Perfect time to experiment. My goals: stretch beyond my OpenAI comfort zone and apply AI tools to something I'm passionate about—coaching hockey. With my son moving to 'A' level in the GTHL, I needed to up my game too.

The challenge was clear: less admin time, better content for higher-level play, and more actual teaching moments instead of searching through PDFs at 11 PM.

**What I built with Claude Code & MCP servers:**

🏒 **Hockey Knowledge Base MCP** - Liberated 1,200+ drills from PDFs into searchable, AI-powered database

📊 **Practice Tracking** - Airtable integration that turns post-practice conversations into data

🎯 **Digital Diagrams** - Natural language to precise hockey diagrams (no more whiteboard photos)

🧠 **Team Learning Platform** - Notion playbook + quiz app for off-ice learning

🔄 **Workflow Automation** - n8n connecting TeamSnap → AI → Notion for enhanced scheduling

**Key takeaways:**
• AI as assistant, not automation - human-led, AI-assisted wins
• MCP tools multiply capability - Hockey knowledge + Claude = helpful. Hockey MCP + Claude = practice plans in seconds
• The gap between idea and implementation has never been smaller

The future isn't AI replacing humans. It's humans with AI tools doing what they do best, just better.
