### Season Plan Overview

#### **Season Duration:** September to March  
#### **Practice Frequency:** 3 times per week  
#### **Key Objectives:**
- Develop team systems (e.g., breakouts, defensive zone coverage)
- Enhance hockey IQ through game scenarios
- Maintain a balanced approach that includes skill development

### Monthly Breakdown

#### **September: Foundations and Evaluation**
- **Focus:** Basic Systems and Player Evaluations
- **Activities:**
  - Evaluate players’ individual skills and readiness
  - Introduce foundational team systems
  - Emphasize communication and teamwork

#### **October: Breakout Systems**
- **Focus:** Offense - Breakouts
- **Activities:**
  - Teach various breakout options
  - Small area games to practice execution
  - Emphasize read-and-react strategies

#### **November: Defensive Zone Coverage**
- **Focus:** Defense - Zone Coverage
- **Activities:**
  - Introduce man-to-man and zone coverage concepts
  - Practice scenarios focusing on decision-making
  - Compete in small games to apply concepts

#### **December: Transition Play**
- **Focus:** Transition between defense and attack
- **Activities:**
  - Drill rapid transitions after turnovers
  - Emphasize quick puck movement and supporting plays
  - Game situation drills to reinforce learning

#### **January: Special Teams**
- **Focus:** Power Play and Penalty Kill Systems
- **Activities:**
  - Teach setups and plays for power play
  - Introduce penalty kill strategies
  - Review previous months' systems in game situations

#### **February: Game Management**
- **Focus:** Situational Play and Game Management
- **Activities:**
  - Train players on managing different game situations (e.g., leading, trailing)
  - Work on breakouts and zone coverage under pressure
  - Play practice games to reinforce lessons learned

#### **March: Playoff Preparation and Team Building**
- **Focus:** Review and Synthesize Learning
- **Activities:**
  - Incorporate all learned systems into full-ice games
  - Focus on execution under game-like conditions
  - Team-building activities to boost morale before playoffs

### Practice Structure

**Each Practice will typically include:**
1. **Warm-Up (10 minutes)**
2. **Skills Focus (30 minutes)**
   - Individual or team skills based on the monthly theme
3. **Systems Application (30 minutes)**
   - Drills to practice specific systems
4. **Game Situation Scenarios (20 minutes)**
   - Small area games or scrimmages to apply concepts
5. **Cool Down & Review (10 minutes)**
   - Wrap-up discussions on lessons learned

### Sample Practice Plan for October (Breakouts)

**Practice Theme:** Breakout Systems

1. **Warm-Up:**
   - Dynamic skating drills (forward/backward, turns)

2. **Skills Focus:**
   - Passing drills with movement (supporting the puck)

3. **Systems Application:**
   - Breakout practice:
     - 2-on-2 breakout vs. forecheck, emphasizing puck movement and support
     
4. **Game Situation Scenarios:**
   - 3-on-3 in a half-ice format, focusing on breakout execution under pressure

5. **Cool Down & Review:**
   - Discuss what went well and areas of improvement

### Communication

- Schedule monthly meetings with parents to keep them updated on progress and expectations.
- Share practice themes and objectives to foster understanding and support at home.

### Checkpoints

- Conduct player evaluations at the end of each month to assess progress and adaptability to systems.
- Seek feedback from players on their comfort level and understanding of systems.
