# Issue #6: Design Team Systems Pages

## Overview
Create 5-6 basic team system explanations for U10 players. Simplify complex hockey systems into visual, easy-to-understand concepts that emphasize fun and teamwork over rigid structure.

## Objectives
- Explain systems in simple, visual terms
- Focus on basic positioning and movement
- Make systems feel like team adventures
- Use 70% visual content with diagrams
- Keep concepts age-appropriate

## Systems to Cover

### 1. 2-1-2 Forecheck
**Simple Name**: "The Pressure Play"
- Two forwards go in to create pressure
- One forward stays high for support
- Two defenders protect our zone
- When to use it and basic rotations

### 2. Basic Breakout - Up the Boards
**Simple Name**: "The Escape Route"
- Defense gets puck and looks for winger
- Center supports in middle
- Simple passing options
- Moving as a unit up ice

### 3. Basic Breakout - Up the Middle
**Simple Name**: "The Fast Break"
- Defense passes to center
- Wingers fly up the boards
- Creating speed through middle
- When this option works best

### 4. Power Play Basics (Umbrella)
**Simple Name**: "The Extra Player Advantage"
- One player at point, two on half-walls
- Two players near net
- Simple passing patterns
- Everyone gets to touch the puck

### 5. Penalty Kill Basics (Box)
**Simple Name**: "The Protection Formation"
- Four players make a box shape
- Protect the middle
- Clear puck when possible
- Work together, stay positive

### 6. Defensive Zone Coverage
**Simple Name**: "Protecting Our House"
- Basic man-to-man concepts
- Staying between opponent and net
- When to pressure vs contain
- Helping teammates

## Visual Style Guidelines
- Use consistent color coding (home team blue, opponents red)
- Simple stick figure players or circles with numbers
- Clear arrows for movement (different colors for puck vs players)
- Whiteboard aesthetic (like coach drawing during practice)
- Include fun elements (stars for goals, smiley faces for success)

## Simplification Strategies
- **2-1-2 Forecheck** → "Two chase, one waits, two protect"
- **Breakout** → "Get puck, pass to friend, skate together"
- **Power Play** → "Extra friend helps us score"
- **Penalty Kill** → "Four friends protect our net"
- **D-Zone Coverage** → "Stay between bad guy and our goalie"
