## Age-Specific Adjustments

### U8 (6-7 years)
- More games, less structure
- 5-7 minute stations
- Focus on fun and basic skills
- 80% activities should be games

### U10 (8-9 years)
- Balance of skills and games
- 10-12 minute stations
- Introduce basic tactics
- 70% games and fun drills

### U12 (10-11 years)
- More structured skill work
- 12-15 minute stations
- Team systems introduction
- 60% skills, 40% games

### U14+ (12+ years)
- Complex skill combinations
- 15-20 minute stations
- Advanced tactics and systems
- 50/50 skills and competitive games

---

## Tips for Success

1. **Arrive Early:** Set up before players arrive
2. **Keep It Moving:** Minimize standing around
3. **Positive Reinforcement:** 3:1 positive to corrective feedback
4. **Demonstrate:** Show, don't just tell
5. **Adapt:** Be ready to modify based on group energy/skill
6. **End on High Note:** Always finish with something fun
