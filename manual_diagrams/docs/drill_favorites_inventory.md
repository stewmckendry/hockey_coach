## Defense Drills

### 1. D-Zone Coverage - 5 on Dice with Phases
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)
- **Time**: 15 minutes
- **Players**: Full Team
- **Equipment**: Pucks, 2 coaches
- **Description**: Progressive D-zone coverage drill. Phase 1: Form 5-on-dice from neutral zone. Phase 2: Right corner battle coverage. Phase 3: Left corner battle coverage. Phase 4: Push the Pace breakout.
- **Key Points**: Quick positioning to 5-on-dice, communication essential, quick transitions on breakout
