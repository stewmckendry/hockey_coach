## Template Implementation Notes

### Content Creation Workflow
1. **Select Template**: Choose appropriate template based on content type
2. **Populate Database**: Fill related database entry with metadata
3. **Customize Content**: Adapt template to specific topic and age group
4. **Visual Integration**: Add diagrams, photos, and visual elements per UX ratios
5. **Review Process**: Check against UX guidelines and safety standards
6. **Publishing**: Apply appropriate permissions and sharing settings

### Template Maintenance
- Templates updated based on analytics feedback
- Seasonal adjustments for different training focuses
- Age group modifications based on developmental research
- Integration with new MCP server capabilities

These templates provide the foundation for the comprehensive content management system while ensuring age-appropriate, safety-focused, and engaging hockey education materials.
