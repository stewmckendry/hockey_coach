# Simple Notion Workspace Structure for Single Hockey Team

## 🏒 Simplified Team Workspace

**Scope**: Single hockey team content management
**Philosophy**: Start simple, evolve as needed

---

## 📁 Core Structure

### 🏠 **Team Home**
*Single landing page for team*

**Content**:
- Team name, age group, season info
- Quick links to most-used pages
- Recent practice plans and updates
- Coach contact info

---

### 📋 **Practice Plans**
*Practice planning and history*

**Subpages**:
- Current week's practices
- Practice plan templates (by age)
- Past practices (archive)

---

### 🥅 **Drills & Skills**
*Complete skill development and tactical training*

**Organization**:
- **Individual Skills**
  - Skating fundamentals
  - Passing & receiving
  - Shooting techniques
  - Puck handling
  - Checking (age-appropriate)
  - Goaltending skills

- **🎯 Team Systems & Tactics**
  - Forechecking systems (1-2-2, 2-1-2, etc.)
  - Defensive zone coverage
  - Breakout patterns
  - Power play setups
  - Penalty kill systems
  - Faceoff plays
  - Neutral zone play

- **Small Area Games**
  - Skill-specific games
  - System application games
  - Competition drills

---

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Parent Corner** 
*Parent-facing information*

**Content**:
- Practice schedule
- Team calendar
- Safety guidelines
- Development tips for home

---

### 📊 **Team Data** (Private)
*Coach-only content and analytics*

**Content**:
- Player development notes
- Source references
- Content performance tracking
- Season planning notes

---

## 🎯 Database Implementation

### Essential Databases Only:
1. **Team Info** - Basic team details
2. **Content Library** - All practice plans, drills, concepts, and systems
3. **Sources** - Reference materials (simplified)

### Optional for Later:
- Full analytics database
- Complex permission systems
- Multi-team structures

---

## 🔧 Getting Started Approach

1. **Phase 1**: Set up Team Home + Practice Plans
2. **Phase 2**: Add Drills & Skills section (individual skills first)
3. **Phase 3**: Add Team Systems & Tactics to Drills & Skills
4. **Phase 4**: Create Parent Corner
5. **Phase 5**: Implement basic analytics
6. **Phase 6**: Evolve based on usage

This simplified structure focuses on core team needs while allowing natural growth as content is created.
