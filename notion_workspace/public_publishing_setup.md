## 🔧 Publishing Workflow

### Content Review Process
1. **Create** content in internal workspace
2. **Review** for privacy and appropriateness
3. **Clean** any team-specific details
4. **Publish** to public Notion site
5. **Share** public links as needed

### Privacy Sanitization
- Remove specific player names
- Generalize team-specific strategies
- Focus on educational value vs. competitive advantage
- Ensure all content benefits broader hockey community
